Site signatures worth presenting

FVN — SMT, not test

9 contamination + 7 solder of 28 records. This is the only larger site where test configuration is not the lead code.

FVT — seating

17 of 21 records are Re-Seat (Wi-Fi antenna, pluggable modules, LED cable). Almost no test-config or packout noise.

FDO — units, not events

16 records but 218 failed units. FA-case mix rather than the FJZ test-line pattern. Do not treat record count as customer impact here.

FJZ — orientation ranks third

Wrong orientation is 27 of 182 at FJZ, ahead of packout (13). Test config is still the bulk at 85.
